03 / How it works

Behind the ranks.

Where do the rankings come from?

Seoul Rising ranks acts using public signals such as YouTube views, Wikipedia pageviews, Naver search trends, and Korean and English news coverage. Tap an act to see the signals behind its ranking.

How often do trends update?

The trend feed updates about every 30 minutes. iOS decides when widgets refresh, so a widget may show an older ranking than the app.

What can I use for free?

The core trend rankings and current widgets are free to use. Future premium features may be offered separately.

Does the app collect my data?

No. The app has no tracking or analytics and doesn’t collect personal data. See the privacy policy for details.
